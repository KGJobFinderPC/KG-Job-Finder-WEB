Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KG Job Finder. This Privacy Policy explains how KG Job Finder collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website and mobile application.

By using KG Job Finder, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KG Job Finder, we may collect the following information:

  • Name or full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • City or location information provided by you.
  • Account type, such as candidate or employer.
  • Job applications and application details.
  • CV files and information contained in CVs uploaded by users.
  • Cover letters and other information voluntarily submitted through application forms.
  • Information required to authenticate your account.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and operate KG Job Finder and its features.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Allow candidates to search and apply for jobs.
  • Allow employers to manage job listings.
  • Allow employers to review applications.
  • Allow authorized employers to access candidate CVs associated with applications.
  • Maintain application and account functionality.
  • Improve the reliability and security of the service.
  • Respond to support requests.

4. Accounts

KG Job Finder allows users to create accounts. Account information is used to provide access to the features associated with the selected account type.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should contact us if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Job Applications

When a candidate submits a job application, information provided in the application may be shared with the employer responsible for the relevant job listing.

This may include the candidate's name, email, phone number, city, CV, cover letter and other information voluntarily provided in the application.

Important: Only submit information that you are comfortable sharing with potential employers through the job application process.

6. CV Files

Candidates may upload CV documents when applying for jobs.

CV files are stored electronically and may be accessed by authorized employers when reviewing applications submitted to their job listings.

Candidates should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in their CV.

7. Data Storage and Service Providers

KG Job Finder uses third-party technology and infrastructure providers to operate parts of the service, including authentication, database functionality and file storage.

Information may therefore be processed and stored using infrastructure provided by these service providers.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that information is handled using appropriate security measures.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should understand that transmission and storage of information over the internet carries some level of risk.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain user accounts, process applications, meet operational requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer required, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the contact details provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a deletion request.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for legitimate operational purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

KG Job Finder is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use online services under applicable law.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

KG Job Finder may use browser storage technologies, including local storage, to support functionality such as maintaining preferences, application state or other necessary application features.

These technologies are used to help the application function correctly and do not necessarily mean that we use them for advertising purposes.

13. Third-Party Links

KG Job Finder may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party services. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service you access.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the service, technology, legal requirements or our practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or a request to delete your data, please contact KG Job Finder.

Email: kgjobfinder@gmail.com

16. Consent

By using KG Job Finder, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to the processing of information as described above.

Delete Your Account

You can request the deletion of your KG Job Finder account and associated personal data by contacting us by email.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your KG Job Finder account and associated personal data, send an account deletion request to:

Account Deletion Email kgjobfinder@gmail.com
Request Account Deletion

Please send the request from the email address associated with your KG Job Finder account whenever possible.

Your request should include enough information for us to identify the account that you want to delete.

2. Data That May Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is approved, we will delete or remove personal data associated with the account, subject to applicable legal, security and operational requirements.

Account Information Name, email address and account-related information.
Profile Information Candidate or employer profile information.
Job Applications Applications associated with the account, where applicable.
CV Files CV files and associated uploaded information.
Cover Letters Cover letters associated with submitted applications.
Other Account Data Other personal information associated with the account, where applicable.

3. Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes.

  • Information required to comply with legal obligations.
  • Information required for security, fraud prevention or abuse prevention.
  • Information necessary to resolve disputes.
  • Information that must be retained for legitimate legal or regulatory reasons.

When information is retained, it will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

4. Account Verification

For security purposes, we may need to verify that an account deletion request was made by the account owner.

We may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify the request before deleting an account.

5. Processing Your Request

After receiving a valid account deletion request, KG Job Finder will review the request and take the appropriate steps to process it.

Processing time may vary depending on the information that needs to be verified and the systems involved.

Important: Sending an email does not automatically delete the account. The request must first be reviewed and verified.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

KG Job Finder uses Supabase for parts of its application infrastructure, including authentication, database functionality and file storage.

Account information, profile information, applications and uploaded CV files may be stored using this infrastructure.

When an account deletion request is processed, applicable account data stored within the KG Job Finder systems may also be removed, subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Contact

If you have questions about account deletion, personal data, or this deletion process, contact KG Job Finder using the email address below.

KG Job Finder kgjobfinder@gmail.com
